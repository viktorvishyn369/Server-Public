PaceSeeker Privacy Policy

StealthLynk — Your data stays on your device

1. Overview

PaceSeeker is designed with a privacy-first, self-custodial architecture. StealthLynk does not centrally collect, store, or process your personal data. All sensitive information remains on your device.

2. Information StealthLynk Does NOT Collect

  • Your Solana private keys or seed phrases
  • Your wallet addresses (unless you voluntarily connect a wallet)
  • Your trading history or balances
  • Your CEX API secrets (stored locally, encrypted)
  • Your personal identity documents
  • Photos, contacts, or device media

3. Information Stored on Your Device Only

All sensitive data is stored locally on your device using platform secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via Expo SecureStore):

  • Session keypairs (encrypted, 24-hour expiry)
  • CEX API keys (if you choose to enter them)
  • App settings and trading preferences
  • Local trade history (CSV exportable by you)

StealthLynk cannot access this data. If you uninstall the App, this data is permanently deleted.

4. Blockchain Interactions

When you enable LIVE mode, transactions are submitted directly to public Solana RPC nodes (e.g., solana-rpc.publicnode.com, api.mainnet-beta.solana.com). These interactions are pseudonymous but publicly visible on the blockchain, as is true for all Solana transactions. StealthLynk does not operate these RPC nodes and has no special visibility into your on-chain activity.

5. CEX API Key Handling

If you enter CEX API keys for live trading, the following applies:

  • API keys and secrets are stored exclusively in device secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore with WHEN_UNLOCKED_THIS_DEVICE_ONLY access level). They are never synced, backed up, or transmitted to StealthLynk.
  • The App communicates only with official exchange API endpoints (e.g., api.bybit.com, www.okx.com, api.mexc.com). There is no intermediary proxy or StealthLynk relay server.
  • Your API secret is never sent over the network — not even to the exchange. The App uses industry-standard HMAC-SHA256 signing: it computes a one-way hash of each request locally using your secret, and transmits only the resulting signature. The exchange verifies it against their stored copy.
  • API keys are used solely for balance reads and market order placement. The App never initiates withdrawals, internal transfers, or account changes unless you explicitly grant Withdraw permission for auto-rebalance.
  • Keys are immediately deleted from secure storage when you tap “Remove” in the App. There is no cache, log, or residual copy.

6. Analytics and Telemetry

PaceSeeker does not include third-party analytics, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs. Error logs, if any, remain on your device unless you manually share them with StealthLynk for support.

7. External Services

The App queries the following external services solely for market data and trade execution:

  • Jupiter Aggregator API — for DEX swap quotes and routing
  • Jito Block Engine — for MEV-protected bundle submission
  • Public CEX APIs — for price feeds (no authentication required)
  • Solana RPC endpoints — for on-chain state and transaction submission

These services may log IP addresses and request data according to their own privacy policies. StealthLynk does not control their data practices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

StealthLynk does not collect personal data centrally. To erase all PaceSeeker data from your device, simply uninstall the App and, if applicable, revoke your session wallet to reclaim any rent SOL.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Even though PaceSeeker does not centrally collect personal data, you retain privacy rights under applicable law. Because all sensitive data is stored locally on your device, StealthLynk does not have access to it and cannot act on requests to access, delete, or modify your data centrally. You can view and manage your locally stored data within the App settings, and you can delete all data by uninstalling the App.

StealthLynk does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. StealthLynk does not use your data for targeted advertising.

10. Data

StealthLynk does not centrally store personal data. Any centrally held data is minimal and non-sensitive.

11. Children

PaceSeeker is not intended for users under 18. StealthLynk does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe data from a child has been inadvertently collected, contact support@stealthlynk.io.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as new versions of the app are released. The current version is always available at this URL.
