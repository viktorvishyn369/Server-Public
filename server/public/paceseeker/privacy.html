PaceSeeker Privacy Policy

StealthLynk LLC — Your data stays on your device

1. Overview

PaceSeeker is designed with a privacy-first, self-custodial architecture. We collect the absolute minimum data necessary, and we never have access to your funds, trading activity, or wallet keys.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Solana private keys or seed phrases
  • Your wallet addresses (unless you voluntarily connect a wallet)
  • Your trading history or balances
  • Your CEX API secrets (stored locally, encrypted)
  • Your personal identity documents
  • Photos, contacts, or device media

3. Information Stored on Your Device Only

All sensitive data is stored locally on your device using platform secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via Expo SecureStore):

  • Session keypairs (encrypted, 24-hour expiry)
  • CEX API keys (if you choose to enter them)
  • App settings and trading preferences
  • Local trade history (CSV exportable by you)

StealthLynk LLC cannot access this data. If you uninstall the App, this data is permanently deleted.

4. Blockchain Interactions

When you enable LIVE mode, transactions are submitted directly to public Solana RPC nodes (e.g., solana-rpc.publicnode.com, api.mainnet-beta.solana.com). These interactions are pseudonymous but publicly visible on the blockchain, as is true for all Solana transactions. We do not operate these RPC nodes and have no special visibility into your on-chain activity.

5. Analytics and Telemetry

PaceSeeker does not include third-party analytics, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs. Error logs, if any, remain on your device unless you manually share them with us for support.

6. External Services

The App queries the following external services solely for market data and trade execution:

  • Jupiter Aggregator API — for DEX swap quotes and routing
  • Jito Block Engine — for MEV-protected bundle submission
  • Public CEX APIs — for price feeds (no authentication required)
  • Solana RPC endpoints — for on-chain state and transaction submission

These services may log IP addresses and request data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect personal data centrally, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our servers. To erase all PaceSeeker data, simply uninstall the App and, if applicable, revoke your session wallet to reclaim any rent SOL.

8. Children

PaceSeeker is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be noted in the App’s release notes. The current version is always available at this URL.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@stealthlynk.io.
