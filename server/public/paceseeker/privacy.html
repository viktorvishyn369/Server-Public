PaceSeeker Privacy Policy

StealthLynk LLC — Your data stays on your device

1. Overview

PaceSeeker is designed with a privacy-first, self-custodial architecture. We collect the absolute minimum data necessary, and we never have access to your funds, trading activity, or wallet keys.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your Solana private keys or seed phrases
  • Your wallet addresses (unless you voluntarily connect a wallet)
  • Your trading history or balances
  • Your CEX API secrets (stored locally, encrypted)
  • Your personal identity documents
  • Photos, contacts, or device media

3. Information Stored on Your Device Only

All sensitive data is stored locally on your device using platform secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore via Expo SecureStore):

  • Session keypairs (encrypted, 24-hour expiry)
  • CEX API keys (if you choose to enter them)
  • App settings and trading preferences
  • Local trade history (CSV exportable by you)

StealthLynk LLC cannot access this data. If you uninstall the App, this data is permanently deleted.

4. Blockchain Interactions

When you enable LIVE mode, transactions are submitted directly to public Solana RPC nodes (e.g., solana-rpc.publicnode.com, api.mainnet-beta.solana.com). These interactions are pseudonymous but publicly visible on the blockchain, as is true for all Solana transactions. We do not operate these RPC nodes and have no special visibility into your on-chain activity.

5. CEX API Key Handling

If you enter CEX API keys for live trading, the following guarantees apply:

  • API keys and secrets are stored exclusively in device secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore with WHEN_UNLOCKED_THIS_DEVICE_ONLY access level). They are never synced, backed up, or transmitted to StealthLynk.
  • The App communicates only with official exchange API endpoints (e.g., api.bybit.com, www.okx.com, api.mexc.com). There is no intermediary proxy or StealthLynk relay server.
  • Your API secret is never sent over the network — not even to the exchange. The App uses industry-standard HMAC-SHA256 signing: it computes a one-way hash of each request locally using your secret, and transmits only the resulting signature. The exchange verifies it against their stored copy.
  • API keys are used solely for balance reads and market order placement. The App never initiates withdrawals, internal transfers, or account changes unless you explicitly grant Withdraw permission for auto-rebalance.
  • Keys are immediately deleted from secure storage when you tap “Remove” in the App. There is no cache, log, or residual copy.

6. Analytics and Telemetry

PaceSeeker does not include third-party analytics, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs. Error logs, if any, remain on your device unless you manually share them with us for support.

7. External Services

The App queries the following external services solely for market data and trade execution:

  • Jupiter Aggregator API — for DEX swap quotes and routing
  • Jito Block Engine — for MEV-protected bundle submission
  • Public CEX APIs — for price feeds (no authentication required)
  • Solana RPC endpoints — for on-chain state and transaction submission

These services may log IP addresses and request data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect personal data centrally, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our servers. To erase all PaceSeeker data, simply uninstall the App and, if applicable, revoke your session wallet to reclaim any rent SOL.

9. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA / GDPR)

Even though PaceSeeker does not centrally collect personal data, we respect the privacy rights granted to you under applicable law. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: You have the right to request what personal information we have collected about you. Because all sensitive data is stored locally on your device, we do not have access to it. You can view your locally stored data within the App settings.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. Since we do not store your data on our servers, the only way to delete it is to uninstall the App, which permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration. We do not use your data for targeted advertising.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.
  • Right to Data Portability (GDPR): EU residents may request a copy of their personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. Since we do not hold your data, you can export your trade history directly from the App via the Tape tab.
  • Right to Object / Restrict Processing (GDPR): EU residents may object to or request restriction of processing of their personal data. Because we do not process your personal data on our servers, these rights are primarily exercised by uninstalling the App or adjusting App permissions on your device.

Exercising Your Rights: To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@stealthlynk.io. We will respond within 45 days (or as required by your jurisdiction). We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

10. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting any centrally stored data (which we currently do not collect), we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable law, including the Wyoming Data Breach Notification Statute (W.S. 40-12-501 et seq.) and any other applicable state or federal law.

11. Cross-Border Data Transfers

PaceSeeker operates from the United States. If you use the App from outside the United States, your data (to the extent any is processed by external services such as RPC nodes or exchange APIs) may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions. These external services operate under their own privacy policies and data processing agreements. We do not control or facilitate cross-border transfers of your personal data.

12. Children

PaceSeeker is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such data.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be noted in the App’s release notes. The current version is always available at this URL.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact support@stealthlynk.io.
