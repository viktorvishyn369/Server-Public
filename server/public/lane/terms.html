Lane

On-device voice companion

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Lane is local-first software — speech recognition, the language model, and speech synthesis run on your device by default. Cloud language models are strictly bring-your-own-key and opt-in. StealthLynk does not see your conversations.

1. About Lane

Lane is a local-first voice companion that runs on your Android device. One button starts a conversation; spoken replies stream back sentence by sentence with interruption (barge-in) support. By default, all AI processing happens on your device:

  • On-device language model — Qwen packs via llama.cpp (no cloud required)
  • Offline speech recognition — Whisper models via sherpa-onnx
  • Offline voices — Kokoro and Piper text-to-speech via sherpa-onnx

Lane also provides optional voice tools (weather, web search, opening apps, setting alarms, dialing calls, emergency numbers, and assistant settings) and an optional Solana wallet integration through Mobile Wallet Adapter.

Lane is not a medical, legal, financial, or emergency service. Responses are generated by AI models and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate for your situation. Do not rely on Lane for decisions that require professional judgment.

2. Your Data Stays on Your Device

Lane is designed so that your conversations and personal data never leave your device:

  • Conversation history, memory (facts, summaries, open threads), settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device
  • Speech is transcribed on-device by default — your voice audio is not sent to StealthLynk servers
  • StealthLynk cannot read your conversations, memory, or voice data
  • If you uninstall the app, all local data is permanently deleted

3. Cloud Models (Optional, Bring-Your-Own-Key)

You may optionally configure a cloud language model by providing your own API key for a supported provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, Together AI, or Mistral). If you do:

  • Your API key is stored only on your device and is never sent to StealthLynk
  • Requests go directly from your device to the provider you selected — StealthLynk is not a relay or intermediary
  • Your conversation content is transmitted to that provider and is subject to that provider's terms and privacy policy
  • You are responsible for your provider account, usage costs, and key security
  • When offline or unconfigured, Lane falls back to the fully local engine

4. Voice Tools

Voice tools are optional features you invoke by asking. By using them you acknowledge:

  • Weather and places — queries are sent to Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap Nominatim; approximate location may be used if you grant location permission
  • Web search — queries are sent to DuckDuckGo
  • Calls — Lane opens your phone dialer with the number filled in; the call is placed and confirmed by you in the dialer app
  • Alarms — Lane opens your clock app's alarm interface via a system intent
  • Emergency numbers — Lane can look up local emergency numbers; this is an information aid only — in an emergency call the official number directly

5. Solana Wallet Features (Optional)

Lane can optionally connect to a Solana wallet via Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA):

  • Wallet connection — used to link your device to your StealthLynk account for subscription and ecosystem features
  • Avatar publishing — you may optionally publish your Lane avatar as a public compressed NFT on Solana mainnet. This is always behind an explicit confirmation and wallet approval — never automatic
  • Self-custody — your private keys never leave your wallet app. Lane never sees, stores, or requests your seed phrase or private keys
  • On-chain actions — blockchain transactions are irreversible once signed and confirmed

You are responsible for the device storage and network usage these downloads require. Removing a pack disables the corresponding local capability until re-downloaded.
