Blipp Account Deletion

How to delete your Blipp data and account

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Blipp is local-first: conversations, memory, settings, and downloaded models live on your device. StealthLynk servers hold only entitlement records (trial, subscription, claims) keyed by device and account identifiers.

1. Delete on-device data

  • Go to Android Settings > Apps > Blipp > Storage > Clear Data, OR
  • Uninstall the app.

This removes all conversations, memory, settings, API keys, and downloaded models stored on the device. There is no server-side copy to delete.

2. Delete server-side account records

Email support@stealthlynk.io and include your ecosystem account ID or the wallet address linked to your account. We will delete your account and entitlement records within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

3. Subscriptions and payments

  • Blipp subscriptions are paid on-chain (SOL, USDC, or SKR) and do not auto-renew — there is nothing to cancel.
  • If you purchased through an app store, cancel the subscription in your store account settings; deleting your Blipp account does not cancel store billing.

4. What is deleted

  • Account ID, device link, and subscription/entitlement records on our servers.
  • All on-device models, settings, memory, and conversations when you clear app data or uninstall.

We do not store your conversations or voice audio on our servers.
