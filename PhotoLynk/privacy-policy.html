Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 20, 2026

1. Service Overview

PhotoLynk ("the Service") is a photo and video backup application developed by StealthLynk, US. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Service.

We are committed to privacy by design: we encrypt your data on your device before it ever leaves, and we cannot access your photos or videos.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local and Remote Modes

Zero-Knowledge Mode: When using Local or Remote modes, all data remains on servers you own and control. StealthLynk, US does not have access to, collect, or store your photos, videos, email address, password, device identifiers, or any file metadata.

You are solely responsible for the security, maintenance, and backup of your self-hosted server and all data stored thereon.

2.2 StealthCloud Mode

When using StealthCloud mode, we collect and process:

  • Account Information: Email address for authentication purposes
  • Device Identifiers: Hardware device identifiers for security and account recovery
  • Encrypted Data: Photos and videos encrypted on your device before transmission
  • Subscription Information: Payment and subscription status (processed by third-party payment processors)
End-to-End Encryption: All media files and metadata are encrypted on your device using industry-standard encryption (XSalsa20-Poly1305 via TweetNaCl) before transmission. StealthLynk, US stores only encrypted data and does not possess the decryption keys. We cannot access, view, or decrypt your files.

2.3 In-App Purchase Information

When you make in-app purchases, we collect and process:

  • Purchase Receipts: Transaction receipts from Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or Solana Mobile dApp Store for verification purposes
  • Account Balance: Credit balance and transaction history for photo certification services
  • Premium Status: Whether you have purchased the Premium tier ($49.99 USD one-time) and associated feature entitlements (100 GB encrypted cloud storage for 4 years, 25 free certifications, 250 commission-free certifications)
  • Verification Credit: Photo Verification Credit purchases ($15.00 USD consumable) that add to your account balance for photo certification services
  • Platform Information: The platform (iOS, Android, or Solana dApp Store) where the purchase was made

Purchase receipts are verified with Apple, Google, or Solana Mobile to prevent fraud and ensure legitimate transactions. We do not store your payment card information — all payment processing is handled by the respective platforms according to their privacy policies.

Transaction records, including purchase amounts, dates, and certification service usage, are retained for accounting, tax compliance, and customer support purposes.

2.4 Blockchain Data

Public Blockchain Data: When using NFT certification features or cryptocurrency payments, certain data is stored on the public Solana blockchain and is permanently visible to anyone.

The following data is stored on the public Solana blockchain:

  • Wallet Address: Your connected Solana wallet address (publicly visible and searchable)
  • Transaction Hashes: Proof of certification timestamp and payment records (publicly visible)
  • Content Hash: SHA-256 hash of your photo content (publicly visible — this is a cryptographic fingerprint, not the photo itself)
  • Certificate Metadata: Title, description, location, camera information, and certification attributes you choose to include
  • Transaction Amounts: SOL or SKR token amounts for payments (publicly visible on the ledger)
Important: This blockchain data is immutable and cannot be deleted by StealthLynk, US or anyone else, as it is part of the decentralized Solana ledger. Even if you delete your PhotoLynk account, blockchain records remain permanently.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: Used only to scan QR codes for server pairing / local setup
  • Photos/Videos: Used to back up your media, restore it, and scan for duplicates
  • Network: Used to connect to your local server, remote server, or StealthCloud
  • Biometric Authentication: Optional, used to secure saved credentials (iOS only)

4. Data Encryption and Security

StealthCloud implements end-to-end encryption:

  • Files are encrypted on your device before upload using TweetNaCl secretbox (XSalsa20-Poly1305)
  • Each file is split into encrypted chunks
  • Encryption keys are derived from your credentials and stored only on your device
  • StealthLynk, US cannot decrypt your data
  • All data transmission occurs over HTTPS/TLS
Security Limitation: However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4.1 Data Integrity

PhotoLynk does not modify, alter, or change any metadata (EXIF data, creation date, location information, camera settings, etc.) of your photos or videos. Your files are stored exactly as they exist on your device, preserving all original properties and metadata intact.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Payment Processing: Apple App Store, Google Play Store, Solana Mobile dApp Store, RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Blockchain Infrastructure: Solana Mainnet blockchain, Jupiter API (token pricing), DexScreener API (token pricing)
  • NFT Storage: IPFS, Arweave, or embedded SVG (depending on user selection)
  • Timestamp Services: RFC 3161 trusted timestamp authorities
  • Provenance: C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity) infrastructure

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

5.1 Blockchain Data Sharing

When you mint an NFT or make a blockchain payment:

  • Your transaction is broadcast to the Solana network and visible to all network participants
  • We share no additional data beyond what is required for the transaction
  • Third-party block explorers may index and display your transaction data

6. Data Retention

  • Local and Remote modes: Data retention is entirely under your control
  • StealthCloud mode: Encrypted data is retained while your subscription is active, plus a 3-day grace period after expiration
  • Account deletion: Upon account deletion, all associated encrypted data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Blockchain data: Permanently retained on the Solana blockchain and cannot be deleted
  • Purchase records: Retained for 7 years for accounting and tax compliance
